Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 October 2026

G-Spark Arcade is a simple browser-game website. This notice explains what this starter version does and does not do. If the site changes, this notice should be reviewed and updated.

Information stored on your device

The site does not require an account. If you choose a nickname, save favourites, or play games that update stats, those values are saved in your browser using local storage on your device. They are not sent to a G-Spark Arcade server by this version. You can clear them by clearing this site's browser storage.

Search and third-party websites

The web-search feature can open Google search results in a new tab. If you follow that link or visit any third-party game site, that provider's own privacy practices and terms apply. G-Spark Arcade does not automatically import or host third-party games.

Hosting logs

The hosting provider used to publish this site may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own policies. Review your chosen host's privacy terms before publishing.

Children and personal information

Do not enter your real name, contact details, passwords, school information, or other private information as a nickname. A nickname is visible only in the browser in this version, but anyone using the same device and browser may be able to see it.

Changes and contact

This notice may need updates if analytics, accounts, advertising, external embeds, or server features are added. No contact address is listed yet; add a monitored public contact address before using the site as a commercial service.
